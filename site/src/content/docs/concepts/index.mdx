Logseq has one idea at its center: everything is a **node** — a block of text or a page — and every node can be linked, tagged, given properties, embedded, and searched. Features like tasks, flashcards, and tables are just tags and properties doing work for you.

Learn these five things and the rest of the app falls into place:

- **[The outliner](/concepts/outliner)** — why block-level editing beats flat documents.
- **[Nodes](/concepts/nodes)** — pages and blocks are the same kind of thing. The single most important concept.
- **[Journals](/concepts/journals)** — one page per day. Where most writing happens.
- **[Links & embeds](/concepts/links)** — `[[double brackets]]` connect everything.
- **[Tags](/concepts/tags)** — group nodes and give them shared properties.
- **[Properties](/concepts/properties)** — typed fields: Text, Number, Date, Checkbox, Url, Node.
- **[Library](/concepts/library)** — optional page hierarchies, if you like folders.
