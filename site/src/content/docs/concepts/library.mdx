The **Library** is a built-in page showing your namespaced pages (`Parent/Child`) as an editable outline. It's Logseq's answer to "where are my folders?": a hierarchy of pages you can rearrange like blocks.

## When to use it

- Pages that share nothing but location — a tree of topics, a manual's chapters — fit namespaces well.
- Pages that share *fields* (status, author, rating) fit [tags](/concepts/tags) better — tags give you tables and queries; namespaces only give you a path.

Most Logseq users skip hierarchy entirely and rely on links and tags. The Library is there if you miss folders.

## Work with it

- **Add existing pages to Library** — button on the Library page; adds the selected page under a namespace.
- Remove an entry by selecting the block and pressing `Backspace` or `Cmd-X` — this removes the page from the hierarchy, not the page itself.
- Convert a top-level block to a page (`#Page` tag) and it lands in the Library namespaced under its parent — a quick way to grow the tree while writing.
